Audit trail

The random_split_manifest.json records training/test donor overlap, selected genes, seeds and SHA-256 fingerprints of each split's cell identifiers. See random_split_metrics.csv for replicate-wise results and donor_held_out/ for the full leave-one-donor-out report.

Limits

Reused test cells across random repetitions are not independent observations. No hypothesis tests or confidence intervals for the between-protocol difference are reported. Changes in donor composition, class support, training size or annotation quality can affect the gap.

Synthetic data are used here only to demonstrate the software; no biological result is implied.
